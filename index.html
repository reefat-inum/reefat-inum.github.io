// 01 · About

Researcher & Educator

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UC Santa Cruz, working in the UCSC Nanoengineering Group under Prof. Ahmet Ali Yanik. My doctoral research is organized around one central engineering challenge: designing transduction platforms that convert physical or chemical signals into measurable outputs with maximum sensitivity and minimum instrumentation complexity.

Over five years I have built plasmonic nanohole array optical sensors, electrochemical impedance biosensors, solid-state nanopore ionic current detectors, and opto-electrochemical electrophysiology systems — each grounded in hands-on system integration, device characterization, and data-driven analysis.

Before UCSC, I served as Lecturer and then Assistant Professor at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET) in Bangladesh, where I taught circuits, power systems, control systems, embedded systems, and DSP to undergraduate engineers. I carry that teaching identity into every aspect of my research — every instrument I have built, every dataset I have analyzed has sharpened my ability to explain not just what the equation says, but why the measurement is hard.

I hold an IEEE membership and UCSC's Equity-Minded Mentoring Certificate, and have co-authored peer-reviewed publications with undergraduate student researchers through UCSC's Science Internship Program.

// 02 · Research

Research Thrusts

🔭

Plasmonic Nanohole Array Optical Sensors

Designed and characterized Fano-resonant NHA optical sensors achieving 100 aM LOD via extraordinary optical transmission spectral transduction. Built complete free-space optical test apparatus from the ground up integrating spectrometers, broadband illumination, and photodetectors.

Nanophotonics · EOT · FDTD
🧪

Electrochemical Sensing Platforms

Engineered a full electrochemical sensing platform using EIS, CV, SWV, and electrophoretic deposition achieving 4.56 aM LOD with 9-log dynamic range. Built Randle's equivalent circuit models and COMSOL FEM simulations to optimize device performance.

EIS · CV · COMSOL FEM
🕳️

Solid-State Nanopore Ionic Current Sensors

Designed and characterized a 700 nm solid-state nanopore achieving 103 aM LOD with 30 dB SNR. Developed MATLAB event detection algorithm for translocation analysis validated across a 6-log calibration range with 100% sensitivity and specificity.

Nanopore · MATLAB · Single-molecule
🧠

ML-Free Voltage Imaging Pipeline

Developed a super-generalized voltage imaging pipeline using OpenCV, ImageJ, and MATLAB with PCA and ICA decomposition. Validated across four biological model systems without dataset-specific tuning — achieving 5× sensitivity improvement over nanoantenna probes.

PCA · ICA · Wide-field imaging
🤖

Deep Learning for Nanophotonic Design

Applied deep learning for forward and inverse design of nanophotonic nanohole arrays, streamlining design for tailored optical functionalities and broadening accessibility of NHA engineering. Published in Nanoscale, 2024.

Deep Learning · Inverse Design · NHA
